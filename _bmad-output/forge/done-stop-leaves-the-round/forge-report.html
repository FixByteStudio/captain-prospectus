A visited stop leaves the round

Started as a stop is done means it's done. It came out as one after-save rule per visit result, a new closed status for Intéressé, and outcome definitions written into the specs. It answers action item 1 of the epic #117 retrospective.

Outcome

This idea has been hardened. After Enregistrer, a visited stop leaves Tournée du jour before and after the sync, unless the agent chooses Aujourd'hui on À relancer or Personne sur place.

ResultAfter EnregistrerComes back
Personne sur placeStep 2: Aujourd'hui (default, goes to the end of the round) or Choisir une date (from tomorrow), plus NotesToday, or on the date
IntéresséOut, with a new closed status interestedNot until a future feature decides
À relancerNothing ticked: Aujourd'hui (end of the round) or Choisir une date (from tomorrow)Today, or on the date (Plus tard)
Pas intéressé · ConvertiOutNever

Locked decisions

  • A new closed status, Intéressé, with interested → interested. It needs an ADR. There is no backfill, because nothing is deployed.
  • À relancer and Personne sur place share one step: Aujourd'hui / Choisir une date, same labels. Aujourd'hui sends today's date for both, so the sync contract is unchanged. The picker disables past days and today.
  • Personne sur place opens on Aujourd'hui, and the radios replace its script questions. À relancer opens with nothing ticked and keeps its questions. Saving it unticked shows Choisissez quand relancer : aujourd'hui ou une date.
  • Hints: Intéressé is Ouvert à la discussion, pas encore inscrit sur la liste d'attente. Converti is Déjà inscrit sur la liste d'attente.
  • Glossary: Waitlist (the sign-up; joining it is a conversion) and Channel (how a prospect joins: today the WhatsApp group or the waitlist). Hints name the commitment, never the channel.

Rejected, and why

  • "Done means done" for every result, including Personne sur place. The owner's own flow needed a same-day revisit.
  • "Out today, back tomorrow" for Intéressé. Those leads wait for a dedicated feature.
  • Mapping Intéressé to Converti, which inflates the conversion rate, or to Pas intéressé, which files warm leads as refusals.
  • Aujourd'hui sending no date: it breaks the required date for À relancer and forces a breaking sync-contract change.
  • Defaulting À relancer to Aujourd'hui: a hurried agent files come back Thursday as today.
  • A post-save dialog: a new component stacked on the save sheet, plus one more screen.
  • The hint pas encore dans le canal. It needs training to decode, "Canal" is a place in Brussels, and it names the channel rather than the commitment.

Weak points that survived

  • A stop that leaves before the sync loses its "Pas encore envoyé" row badge. Only the band's pending count still shows the unsent visit.
  • Intéressé leads now leave every agent's round, and nothing works them until the future feature ships.
  • The phone must never reason in statuses. The chosen result drives only the phone's next step (invariant 3), and the implementation has to hold that line.
