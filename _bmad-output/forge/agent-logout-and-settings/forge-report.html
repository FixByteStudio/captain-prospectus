The field account card

Started as "agents have no logout button, and a mini settings page for the theme". It ended with no logout and one small card under the avatar.

Locked

  • No agent logout. Signing back in needs a fresh one-time code from an admin, so a stray tap strands the agent. The admin's « Révoquer » already ends a device's session.
  • Tapping the avatar opens a small card under it: a native popover with a shadcn look. It closes on a tap outside, a tap on the avatar, or Escape.
  • The email is never cut off. 12px, the card widens to fit it, and a long email wraps before « @ ». Then the role.
  • Apparence: Système / Clair / Sombre as native radios with monitor / sun / moon icons. Système is the default and clears the stored choice. A choice applies at once.
  • The dashboard moves into the card for an online admin, behind the leave guard. The tab bar is four tabs for everyone.
  • Version 0.1.0 · a605114: the release plus the deploy commit, written in by CI (« · dev » locally), in its own commit.
  • One story, no epic, no ADR. Published as GH #561.

Rejected, and why

  • Agent « Se déconnecter »: getting back in needs an admin's code.
  • A colour picker: the tokens' contrast tests forbid custom colours.
  • A settings page or bottom sheet: too big for what it holds.
  • A « demandez un code » line: cut by the owner.
  • A shadcn DropdownMenu: extra weight, and its radios aren't native.
  • Keeping the dashboard tab: the horizontal tab bar is the crowded space, and a vertical card has room.
  • A date in the version line: the release plus the commit says more.

Weak points that survived

  • Native popover needs iOS 17+ or a recent Chrome. The agents' iOS versions haven't been checked yet.
  • An unsaved visit form is not stored anywhere (only saved visits reach the outbox). The dashboard button has to go through the leave guard, or a visit in progress gets lost.
  • The theme setting is one per phone, shared with the admin screens.
  • Sally's rule: the card holds nothing without a reason, or it becomes the next crowded tab bar.
