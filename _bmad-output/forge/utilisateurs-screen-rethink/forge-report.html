Utilisateurs screen rethink

The owner was not satisfied with #594's two stacked tables, Agents then Administrateurs.

Outcome

Hardened. The layout idea was wrong, not the build. It is replaced by two tabs over one table.

Locked decisions

  • Tabs « Agents n » (default) / « Administrateurs n », one table under the selected tab, no role badge.
  • First column header « Nom », so « Utilisateurs » appears only in the page title.
  • « Désactivés (n) » per tab, under that tab's list.
  • After adding a user, the screen opens the tab their row lands in.
  • Phone: full-width tabs in two equal halves over today's list rows. An empty Agents tab keeps its muted line.

Rejected

  • Two stacked sections (#594): two frames, two header rows and two toggles for about five people. Too heavy.
  • One table with an Agent/Admin badge: shows everyone at a glance, but the owner wants admins out of the way.
  • « Nom et e-mail »: accurate, but long. « Email » would be English, and « & » is informal.
  • Per-tab labels « Agent » / « Administrateur »: they just repeat the tab.

Weak points that survived

  • Admins sit one click away. This is accepted on purpose.
  • Whether the tab goes in the URL is not decided. The assumption is no, with Agents on every visit.

Who pressure-tested it

🎨 Sally, UX Designer (lead)Weighed screen chrome against data, and checked that agent and admin rows have the same shape. Proposed « Nom » and the switch to the new row's tab.
🧾 Odile, fleet manager at a two-agent outfit (outside voice)"I open this page twice a month. I think in people, not roles." She pointed out that keeping admins hidden is a feature.
