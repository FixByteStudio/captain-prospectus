The admin sidebar footer: the recap card

Forge session · captain-prospectus · 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-14 · outcome: hardened

What was forged

A question about a footer that the mockups drew and the build silently dropped: what should « Synchronisé à l'instant » mean for an admin, who has no outbox? It ended as a small-font recap card that tells the admin the day's story before they dive in.

● 1 / 2 agents actifs aujourd'hui
  12 visites aujourd'hui
  Dernière visite reçue il y a 4 min
admin@exemple.be

Locked decisions

  • A recap card on every admin page, capped at three lines and the email. It is not a dashboard.
  • The headline is « N / M agents actifs aujourd'hui ». Actif means a visit received today (G6), and M is the active roster. It needs no clock, so « 1 / 2 » flags a problem at 11:00 and « 2 / 2 » reads as a good day at 19:00.
  • Below it, the visits today and the time since the last visit was received.
  • The email is informative only, with no menu. The top-bar avatar menu stays.
  • In the icon rail: initials, a pulse dot and a tooltip. In the phone drawer: the full card.
  • One small admin read and one 15 s poll. No agent-side or sync contract change, and nothing added to the phones' precache.

Rejected, and why

  • « Synchronisé à l'instant »: an admin has no sync, so a green dot would describe a browser refresh while an agent sits offline.
  • « en tournée » (present tense): it needs working hours nobody defines.
  • Account menu in the footer: the email is there to inform, not to act.
  • Build line: there is no build id yet, and no menu to hold one.

Weak points that survived

  • « Actif aujourd'hui » counts an agent from their first received visit. A phone that works offline all morning shows up late.
  • The footer's poll is a new request stream for every open admin tab. Its cost has not been measured.

Spun out: its own epic

Dated rounds grouped by date, with X stops and the number left. A mandatory « Commencer la tournée » swipe that reveals the round, and « Terminer la tournée ». A screen listing every round, including done visits. Hours worked per round as a stat. It needs ADRs for a new agent insert type, a sync contract bump and a working-time record.
