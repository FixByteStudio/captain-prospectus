Pas intéressé : une raison, pas le script

Forge · Captain Prospectus · 30 septembre 2026

Issue

A prospect who refuses won't answer a whole questionnaire. From now on, a Pas intéressé visit skips the script's questions, and the agent records one refusal reason with a single tap. Because refusals no longer answer the script, the reason is the only record of why Brussels says no.

La règle

A refusal reason is something the pitch can't answer on the spot. If the pitch has an answer, the agent should have given it, and it's not a reason.

"Pas le temps" and "Voir avec le patron" aren't refusals. They go to À revoir.

Écarté

  • Full script for refusals: nobody who refuses answers six questions.
  • Free text only: can't be counted, and agents end up typing "pas intéressé".
  • An admin-edited list: it's a one-question script, so the script machinery comes back.
  • Contre les commissions: 0 % commission on dishes is the pitch.
  • Déjà sur les plateformes: a situation, not a reason. There's no exclusivity.
  • Ne livre pas: delivery isn't live yet. Click and collect and QR at the table are for these places.

Points qui tiennent encore

  • Captain Food takes Brussels restaurants, which the owner confirmed. The research still says Tours only.
  • Is the reason required? The owner leans towards one required tap.
  • Should out_of_target flag the prospect for the admin?
  • Additive sync field, and enforceRequired changes on both client and server. Check whether this needs an ADR under ADR-0024.
