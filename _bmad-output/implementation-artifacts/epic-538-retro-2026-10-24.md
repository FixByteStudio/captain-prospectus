### Worker and shared (adversarial and edge-case lenses)

No blocker. The adversarial lens found invariants 4, 5 and 12 intact under duplicated, late and racing syncs for visits, tournées and stops; the edge-case lens walked clock skew, retries, two phones, « Nouveau » and boundary values and found the issues below. Checked and clean is listed in the last bullet of this section.

**N1. A phone whose Brussels day differs from the server's is sent back to the start screen after it swipes.** *Confirmed by reading.* Medium. Defer to a story.
- The server files a tournée under the day of `min(startedAt, now)` (`src/worker/tournee.ts:42-46`) and the pull answers for the server's `now` (`:141`). The phone compares the pulled `dayStart` with its own clock's day (`src/client/field/tournee-gate.ts:45-48`, `src/client/field/tournee-stops.ts:175`) and replaces its cache wholesale (`src/client/field/sync.ts:274-282`).
- Sequence: a phone ahead by a day, or by minutes near server midnight, swipes. The server clamps the swipe to `now`, so it lands on day D. The outbox row is accepted and deleted. The pull returns day D while the phone is on D+1, so `swipedToday` is false and the gate shows the start screen again. Each further swipe stores one more tournée on D. A phone behind the server across midnight loses its tournée the other way (`todayTournee` returns null for the new day).
- Visits clamp the same way: a visit made at phone 00:01 with the server at 23:59 is filed on D and drops out of the phone's counter.
- Not caused by this epic: it is the family of #496's unverified bullet U1. ADR-0036:100 accepts a skewed clock *winning* the race; it does not accept it trapping the gate. The epic's Done-when 2 pins "both clocks ahead" only inside one Brussels day (`src/worker/sync.test.ts:1749`).
- Prevent: a skew test that crosses midnight, whenever a spec says "whatever the phone's clock".

**N2. The feed cursor can still skip a visit that commits late.** *Confirmed by reading; likelihood low.* Defer, and schedule the enabler story.
- `received_at` is stamped once, at the start of the sync request (`src/worker/routes/agent.ts:69`, `:152`), and the cursor compares `coalesce(repaired_at, received_at)` (`src/worker/door-days.ts:27`, `:182`). Sync S1 starts at 1000 and commits at 1800; S2 starts at 1200 and commits at 1300; a poll at 1400 lifts the cursor to 1200; S1's visits sit below it until a reseed. Both the adversarial (A1) and edge-case (E5) lenses found it independently.
- R4 closes the *tie* gap only, which is what the epic promised. This one was deferred in `deferred-work.md:432-435` (#540) and again in `:447-449` (#528): the same reason in two stories, and no issue exists for either. CLAUDE.md schedules the enabler story instead of a third deferral.
- Prevent: the two-deferral rule needs someone to count; the retro is that someone.

**N3. Reason-filtered delta polls can fail to advance.** *Real in the code, no test; unlikely in practice.* Accept the risk, answer the bot, add a test with the enabler of N2.
- `doorDaysTouched` reads `limit + 1` visits that carry the reason, then drops door-days whose *latest* visit lacks it (`src/worker/door-days.ts:322-325`). If every touched visit belongs to a dropped door-day, the poll returns no rows, `nextSince` (`src/client/admin/visits/feed.ts:43`) cannot move, and the next poll re-reads the same window. That takes limit-many superseded refusals after the cursor within one open tab; a reseed clears it.
- CodeRabbit raised exactly this on PR #546 as **Major** at `door-days.ts:190`; nobody replied (see N5). No test covers `reason=` with a truncated delta poll (`src/worker/admin.test.ts:2078` and `:2256` use single, non-truncated polls).

**N4. Stop numbers can be reassigned between phones while a swipe's stops are still draining.** *Mechanism confirmed by reading; needs a swipe over 250 stops, or a throw between the tournée insert and the stops insert.* Defer, low.
- A swipe sends at most `SYNC_STOPS_PER_REQUEST` = 250 stops per request (`src/shared/constants.ts:176`). A second phone that pulls the tournée holds 250 stops and re-appends the unsent ones as its own joiners numbered from `max + 1` (`src/client/field/tournee-joiners.ts:34-54`). If those reach the server first, the unique `(tournee_id, number)` index drops the first phone's real stops, which are still listed in `accepted` because the insert has no conflict target (`src/worker/tournee.ts:113`, `:119`). The first phone is renumbered on its next pull, against ADR-0036's "numbers do not change".
- The same applies when the tournée row is stored and its stops are not (edge-case E4). A single phone does not hit it: its own queued stops count as held (`tournee-stops.ts:177-181`).

**N5. A loser's joiner whose prospect the visit closed is never re-appended.** *Confirmed by reading.* Defer, low.
- The comment at `src/worker/tournee.ts:99-102` says the loser's next pull re-appends the dropped prospect. `snapshotProspects` only offers open prospects (`src/shared/tournee.ts:277-278`), and a door-day counts only when the tournée holds a stop for it (`tournee-stops.ts:214-226`). A joiner visited and closed before the collision resolves is in neither counter total nor Carnet number.

**N6. The `try/catch` around `writeTournees` hides a permanent failure.** *Confirmed by reading.* Accept, with a note.
- It logs only the error name (`src/worker/routes/agent.ts:274-279`), deliberately (a Drizzle message carries bound values). A deterministic failure answers 200 for every sync, resends up to 7 tournées and 250 stops each time (the #565 cost), and leaves the admin round and `agentsStartedOn` at zero. Nothing is lost: `accepted` is all-or-nothing on a throw. No realistic poison input was found.

**N7. The winning tournée can change during the day, and nothing moves the loser's stops.** *Confirmed, and documented.* Accept (ADR-0036:97-102, amended 2026-10-17). A phone that swiped earlier but synced at 11:00 displaces the one the other agent has been walking; the displaced stops are stored and never read, and visits carry no tournée id.

**N8. `doorDayCounts` reads every visit of up to 200 prospects into JS** (`src/worker/door-days.ts:337-360`, called from `src/worker/routes/admin.ts:1263`) and runs `Intl` per distinct day. *Unmeasured, not shown to be a defect.* Defer: measure it, as #496 measured its other CPU figures, and add a row to `docs/free-tier-budget.md` (invariant 13).

**Checked and clean** (both lenses, read on main, no test run by them): every id in `accepted.stops` and `accepted.tournees` is either stored or deliberately drained (a foreign tournée, or a prospect that never came after the tournée's day); a throw in `writeTournees` lists none of its ids, and every insert is `onConflictDoNothing`, so the resend is a no-op; visits are acknowledged before it; all readers (phone pull, admin round, recap, dashboard) go through `dayStart`, `todayTournee` or `agentsStartedOn`; `startedAt` and `addedAt` are clamped to `now` (invariant 12) and the unclamped `clientStartedAt` only ranks; the bound-parameter counts are 96 (tournées), 98 (stops), 51 (owned lookup) and 100 (prospect lookup) (invariant 7); DST and the 00:00–01:00 hour in `src/shared/period.ts`; the tie-group take and the `<= newest` bound of the feed. Not covered by the lenses: the Dexie migration and `confirmOutbox` flows beyond how `sync.ts` and `tournee-stops.ts` use them, `deriveProspectStatus`, the `doorDaysBetween` and `doorDaysOn` SQL offset logic (the verification lens covers it through tests), and the `visits_listed_idx` expression match.

**Considered and set aside:** E3 (a swipe over `TOURNEE_STOP_NUMBER_MAX` = 10,000 stops would 400 the whole sync; unreachable before the pull payload exhausts the CPU budget); duplicate numbers or prospects inside one request (only a buggy client can send them); the pre-upgrade Dexie window below, which the story specs accepted.
