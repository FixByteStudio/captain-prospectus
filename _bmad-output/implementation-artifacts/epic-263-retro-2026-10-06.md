#### F4: a phone clock that goes backwards mid-day blocks later readings (low, accepted)

- **What happens.** The upsert only replaces a row whose stored `captured_at` is older (`agent-position.ts`, `setWhere`), and it compares the phone's time as sent. The phone guards the next-day case, a fix dated after today, but a clock set back inside the same day makes every later reading look older, so the admin sees an old position until the phone's time passes it.
- **Why accepted.** ADR-0028 prescribes the compare "even from a phone whose clock runs ahead", and the 5.2 security-reviewer's low on this was rejected with that reason (`spec-5-2-…`, row 9). The effect is a stale dot with its time shown, not lost data.
- **Disposition.** Accept as-is; record here so later retros stop re-flagging it.
