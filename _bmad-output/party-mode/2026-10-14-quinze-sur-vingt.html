It opened with an owner, furious: Karim had saved sixteen visits and the screen said "0 visites sur n aujourd'hui". Then every save brought the count back to 1. Then, it turned out, a second knock on the same door could push it to 17 sur 16.

By the end of the next day the room had found both bugs, the first one was fixed, and the tournée had a new shape. Numbers frozen at a morning swipe, one door counted once, and a new tab called Carnet for everything Karim has done today.

Watch the phone. That's the whole session in nine seconds.

How it unfolded

  1. 🔍

    The count lived on the phone

    The counter was a phone-only log plus the outbox. A throwaway test proved the log worked when nothing touched it, but being logged out or another agent signing in on the phone wiped it. Prospects came back with the next pull and the log never did.

    Amelia: That symptom fits one picture exactly: the log adds nothing and only the outbox counts.

  2. 🫥

    A screen with no memory

    Closed outcomes vanished from the round, dated follow-ups slid into Plus tard. The counter was the only trace of the morning, and it was the part that broke.

    Sally: We decided it leaves the walking order. We never gave it anywhere else to go.

  3. 🧮

    "My bad": a second bug

    The owner spotted it: À relancer and Personne sur place sent Karim back to the same door, and n climbed past m. Visit ids on top, prospect ids underneath, and a comment in the code hiding the overflow on the bar. Filed as #492, fixed in #494 before the session ended.

    Sally: Count portes, not visites.

  4. 📜

    Edit? Winston pulls ADR-0007

    The owner pitched an epic: tournées by date, a morning swipe, a screen for the day, reopen, edit. Winston read line 26 aloud: agents cannot edit a visit; corrections are a new visit. Model B was born: keep every visit, group the reads.

    Winston: The bug is in the counting, not in the storage.

  5. 🤨

    "How the heck can two agents be on the same round?"

    The owner caught Winston out. A prospect has one assigned_to. Winston conceded on the spot.

    John: So your "two agents could edit the same visit" argument against model A was thinner than you made it sound.

  6. 📓

    Carnet

    "Ma journée" sounded like a wellness app. Karim picked the name.

    Karim: Carnet. C'est ce que j'avais dans la poche avant votre app.

  7. 👉

    The swipe becomes the door

    John tried to cut the morning swipe. The owner overruled him: no swipe, no tournée, for the whole app.

    Karim: Un swipe le matin, comme pointer. Ça va. Tant que ça marche sans réseau.

  8. 🔢

    Arrêt, frozen at the swipe

    Every saved stop counts once, whatever the outcome, and moves to Carnet. Sally's split counter was erased in favour of one plain number. Then the owner froze the numbers at the swipe, ordered from where Karim stood.

    Karim: Quinze sur vingt. Voilà. Merci.

  9. 📍

    GPS opens the day, it doesn't police it

    No position, no swipe. A full screen became an alert dialog with one button, "Activer", and Amelia's fallback paragraph was struck through.

    Winston: Boss, you're getting dangerously good at boring.

  10. ✅

    ADR-0036, accepted

    "A morning swipe freezes each agent's numbered tournée". It amends ADR-0028 and leaves ADR-0007 untouched. Proposed and approved the same day.
