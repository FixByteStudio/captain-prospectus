« 15 sur 20 » counts arrêts with at least one visit today; a second visit to a stop does not count again. The card reads the lowest-numbered stop with no visit today. Numbers are frozen at the swipe: 19 was reassigned before its visit, so it leaves a gap and the total; 21 joined after the swipe and carries « Nouveau » (an outline badge, never the status badge). Every saved stop leaves Tournée, whatever its outcome; « Plus tard » is gone. Frame 3 is the end of the day (an admin's bar: five tabs). Carte pins carry the same numbers.
