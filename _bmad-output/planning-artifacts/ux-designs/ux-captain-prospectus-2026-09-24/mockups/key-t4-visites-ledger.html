T4 — Visites : une ligne par prospect et par jour (entrée 11)

A door-day is one agent, one prospect and one Brussels day; its row shows the latest visit's time and outcome, a flyer if any visit gave one, and « {n} visites » when there is more than one. Opening a row expands it in place (no dialog) and lists every visit of that door-day, oldest first, with its note and answers. Visits are never edited. « 312 visites » and the strip count door-days (entry 10). The CSV export stays one row per visit.
