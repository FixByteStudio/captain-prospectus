T1 — Avant le départ (entrées 3, 4)

Story 1.1 (#497), ADR-0036 › The gates. Sign-in → no tournée (two messages) or start screen → location alert dialog until a reading is precise enough → swipe « Commencer la tournée ». No tab bar before the swipe: the app opens after it. The sync is never gated: the band dot and the unsent-visits strip show here too (frame 4). The swipe is also a real button « Commencer la tournée » for the keyboard and screen readers; it reuses swipe.ts's axis lock (10px slop) and commits past three quarters of the track; a pointer tap only nudges the thumb.
