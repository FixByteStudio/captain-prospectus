T3 — Carnet et minuit (entrées 8, 9)

Carnet lists today's door-days, newest first: number, name, latest outcome (a neutral badge, never a status colour), time. Tapping a row opens the visit form and saves a new visit; the counter does not move. Closed prospects stay listed. Carnet shows today only. At Brussels midnight the gates come back: a visit form open at 00:00 saves first, then the start screen of the new day shows under the location dialog (frame 3). A door-day whose prospect is not in today's tournée (a visit saved at 00:01 on a stop that then closed) shows without a number and does not count (frame 4).
